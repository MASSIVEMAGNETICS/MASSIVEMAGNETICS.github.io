Why this is different

The output is not “what the AI thinks.” It is what the evidence can carry.

Truth Compiler behavior

  • Extracts bounded claims before judging them.
  • Binds each material conclusion to evidence.
  • Separates observation, inference, prediction, and authority.
  • Preserves contradictory evidence instead of smoothing it away.
  • Leaves unsupported questions UNKNOWN.
  • Can revise a first-pass inference when deeper evidence explains the signal.
  • Returns an audit receipt that records scope and limitations.

What it refuses to do

  • Invent missing evidence.
  • Use a confidence score as proof.
  • Declare production readiness just because code exists.
  • Declare financial causation from correlation alone.
  • Hide evidence that weakens the preferred narrative.
  • Turn customer secrets into public examples without permission.
  • Claim certainty outside the available evidence.

Actual customer value

Different evidence domains. Same truth discipline.

Repository / technical

Know whether a project actually contains the architecture, tests, integrations, controls, release evidence and authority boundaries it claims. Useful for founders, buyers, agencies, investors and engineering teams.

Personal financial reality

Map authorized statements or exports into income/inflow structure, recurring charges, fees, debt pressure, anomalies, leakage and unresolved questions—without pretending to be a CPA audit or investment adviser.

Business financial reality

Separate revenue, cash conversion, recurring obligations, concentration, margin pressure, anomalies and causal hypotheses from what the underlying records actually prove.

Knowledge capital

Rank candidate ideas and assets by evidence, novelty signal, prior art, demonstrated versus hypothetical status, commercial application, defensibility and leverage—with legal/IP boundaries explicit.

Remediation

Move from finding to verified correction: fix the highest-impact bounded issues, rerun evidence checks and record the new state instead of closing the job at “here is a report.”

Continuous governance

Re-scan a repository over time for evidence drift, new contradictions, claim changes, CI/runtime changes and unresolved risks so yesterday's PASS does not silently become today's UNKNOWN.
