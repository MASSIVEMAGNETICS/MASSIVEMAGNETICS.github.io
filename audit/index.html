Why this is different

Semantics first. Evidence second. Authority last.

Truth Compiler behavior

  • Separates discovery from evidence.
  • Separates prediction from proof.
  • Preserves contradictions instead of smoothing them away.
  • Uses structure recognition to find hidden dependencies and mismatches.
  • Returns bounded conclusions with an evidence trail.

What it does not do

  • Invent evidence to complete a narrative.
  • Turn an LLM confidence score into a fact.
  • Declare production authorization from a heuristic ranker.
  • Hide unknowns because they are inconvenient.
  • Guarantee that external-world claims are true beyond supplied evidence.
