2. Evidence states are not guarantees

Truth Compiler separates observed evidence from inference and unknowns. A PASS, VERIFIED, PRESENT, PARTIAL, FAIL, UNSUPPORTED, CONFLICTING, REVIEW-ONLY, or UNKNOWN state is a conclusion about the evidence available within the stated scope and time—not a universal guarantee about every possible external fact, future event, hidden system, undisclosed record, or undiscovered vulnerability.

Governing rule: prediction is not proof. Missing evidence remains missing. Contradictions remain visible. Heuristic or model output alone does not create production authorization, legal rights, financial assurance, or factual certainty.
