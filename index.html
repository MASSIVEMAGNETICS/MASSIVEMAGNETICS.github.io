THE MUSIC ISN'T A COSTUME. IT'S THE RECEIPT.

IAMBANDOBANDZ is Brandon Emery: an independent rapper, producer, visual creator, founder, and systems builder from Lorain, Ohio.

The records turn Rust Belt poverty, homelessness, grief, addiction, family pressure, betrayal, fatherhood, survival, and technological obsession into cinematic transmissions for people who have been ignored, underestimated, or counted out.

Bando is the survivor. IAMBANDOBANDZ is the declaration that the survivor became the author, the label, the broadcast tower, and the machine builder.

“The signal does not need permission to exist.”
