Control law / glossary

WHAT THIS MEANS

Preservation law: Execution can terminate while knowledge is preserved. A road-closed branch retains provenance, evidence, stop reason, dependencies, relationships and reactivation conditions.

CANDIDATE

Captured and preserved, but not consuming an ACTIVE slot.

ACTIVE

One of at most three execution commitments. Human attention is the scarce resource.

VAULTED

Potentially valuable, but not executable now. Preserve it with a reactivation trigger instead of letting it rot in memory.

MERGED

The branch stopped independently because its value was absorbed into another canonical branch. The original record remains.

KILLED

Evidence says the approach is invalid, dominated, or not worth pursuing. KILLED is not deletion: the tombstone prevents repeating expensive mistakes.

SHIPPED

A completed artifact reached the outside world. Add a receipt or URL.

MONETIZED

Verified economic value exists. Record the amount/date/context rather than treating projected revenue as realized revenue.

Priority heuristic

Higher impact/evidence/revenue/completion increase priority; higher cost/time/dependency burden reduce it. The score helps triage. It does not decide truth or guarantee value.

Closure rate

Share of preserved ideas that reached SHIPPED, MONETIZED, MERGED, or KILLED. VAULTED is preserved opportunity, not closure proof.

Estimated portfolio value

Sum of your own expected-value entries. It is planning metadata—not an appraisal, investment value, or realized revenue.

Reactivation trigger

A concrete condition or date that makes a VAULTED/CANDIDATE branch worth reviewing again.

Transcript provenance

Raw transcript text can be preserved, but uncertain names/entities remain UNVERIFIED and cannot silently rewrite canonical history.
