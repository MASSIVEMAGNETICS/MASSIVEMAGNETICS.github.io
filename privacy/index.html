PRIVACY

Effective August 14, 2026

Core rule: collect only what serves the signal, document consent, and do not sell audience data.

Information you provide

The site may let you submit an email address, mobile number, and SMS consent before continuing to music or other destinations. A phone number is not submitted through the current capture form unless the SMS consent box is checked.

Interaction data

The site records limited interaction events in your browser, such as event name, clicked destination, page path, and timestamp. The current client code keeps up to 250 such events in localStorage and uses a sessionStorage flag to avoid repeatedly showing the signal-capture prompt in the same session.

Service providers

Capture submissions are currently relayed through FormSubmit to the IAMBANDOBANDZ contact mailbox. The site's event interface can also send events to analytics providers such as Google Analytics, Plausible, or Umami when those services are configured. External music, social, distribution, and development links are governed by those services' own policies.

How information is used

Submitted contact information may be used for releases, videos, major transmissions, direct communication, bookings, features, press, partnerships, and related IAMBANDOBANDZ/B Heard Network activity. Consent records should be retained with enough provenance to prove the channel, source, text version, and grant or revocation event.
